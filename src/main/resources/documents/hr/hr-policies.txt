CLOUDFLOW INC. — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & HR POLICIES
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
=================================================

SECTION 1: EMPLOYMENT BASICS
------------------------------
1.1 Employment Type
CloudFlow offers full-time, part-time, and contract employment. Full-time employees work 40 hours per week. Part-time employees work 20-30 hours per week. All employees are "at-will" unless otherwise specified in a written employment agreement.

1.2 Probationary Period
New employees undergo a 90-day probationary period. During this time, performance is closely monitored with check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days. Benefits (except health insurance) begin after successful completion of the probationary period.

1.3 Work Schedule
Standard business hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday. CloudFlow supports flexible scheduling — employees may adjust their hours with manager approval, provided they maintain 4 hours of overlap with their team's core hours (10 AM - 2 PM).

SECTION 2: COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
------------------------------------
2.1 Pay Schedule
Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays via direct deposit. Pay stubs are available in the HR portal (hr.cloudflow.io) 2 days before payday.

2.2 Health Insurance
Full-time employees are eligible for health, dental, and vision insurance from day one. CloudFlow covers 90% of employee premiums and 75% of dependent premiums. Plans include PPO and HMO options through Aetna and United Healthcare.

2.3 Retirement (401k)
CloudFlow offers a 401(k) plan with a 4% employer match (vested after 2 years). Employees may contribute up to IRS annual limits. A Roth 401(k) option is also available.

2.4 Stock Options
Full-time employees receive stock option grants as part of their offer. Options vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Additional grants may be awarded based on performance and tenure.

2.5 Professional Development
Each employee receives a $2,500 annual learning budget for courses, conferences, books, and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over. Requests are approved by direct managers through the HR portal.

SECTION 3: PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
--------------------------------
3.1 PTO Allowance
- Years 1-3: 20 days (160 hours) per year
- Years 4-7: 25 days (200 hours) per year
- Years 8+: 30 days (240 hours) per year
PTO is accrued bi-weekly. Maximum accrual cap is 1.5x annual allowance.

3.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 sick days per year, separate from PTO. Unused sick days roll over up to a maximum of 30 days. A doctor's note is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

3.3 Company Holidays
CloudFlow observes 11 paid holidays: New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (2 days), Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day.

3.4 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of paid leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Part-time return schedules can be arranged.

3.5 Bereavement Leave
Up to 5 days for immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling). Up to 3 days for extended family. Additional unpaid leave may be requested through HR.

SECTION 4: REMOTE WORK POLICY
-------------------------------
4.1 CloudFlow is a remote-first company. All positions are remote-eligible unless specified otherwise in the job posting.

4.2 Remote employees must maintain a dedicated workspace with reliable internet (minimum 25 Mbps download). CloudFlow provides a $1,000 home office setup stipend for new employees and $500 annually for equipment upgrades.

4.3 Remote employees must be available during core collaboration hours (10 AM - 2 PM in their team's primary time zone) and responsive on Slack during working hours.

4.4 International remote work is supported in approved countries. Employees must notify HR at least 30 days before relocating internationally. Tax implications and work authorization are the employee's responsibility.

SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT
----------------------------
5.1 CloudFlow is committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free workplace. Discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any protected characteristic is strictly prohibited.

5.2 All employees must complete annual training on anti-harassment, diversity & inclusion, and information security. Training is tracked via the HR portal.

5.3 Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR and the employee's manager. This includes outside employment, investments in competitors, and personal relationships that may affect business decisions.

5.4 Confidential information (trade secrets, customer data, financial data) must be protected at all times. Violations may result in immediate termination and legal action.

SECTION 6: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
-----------------------------------
6.1 Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually (June and December). Reviews include self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback (360-degree).

6.2 Performance is rated on a 5-point scale: Exceptional (5), Exceeds Expectations (4), Meets Expectations (3), Needs Improvement (2), Unsatisfactory (1).

6.3 Employees rated "Needs Improvement" receive a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with specific, measurable goals. Failure to meet PIP goals may result in termination.

6.4 Promotions are based on demonstrated performance, skills growth, and business need. Promotion cycles occur in Q1 and Q3.

SECTION 7: EXPENSE POLICY
---------------------------
7.1 Business travel requires pre-approval from direct manager for expenses over $500. Flights should be booked economy class for domestic travel. Business class is permitted for international flights over 6 hours.

7.2 Meals during business travel: up to $75/day in the US, up to $100/day internationally. Alcohol is limited to $25 per meal with clients.

7.3 Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days via Expensify (integrated with the HR portal). Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. Late submissions may not be reimbursed.

SECTION 8: LEAVING CLOUDFLOW
------------------------------
8.1 Voluntary resignation requires 2 weeks written notice (4 weeks for directors and above). Notice should be submitted to the direct manager and HR simultaneously.

8.2 Upon departure, employees must return all company equipment within 5 business days. Final paycheck includes unused PTO balance. Stock option exercise periods are defined in the Stock Option Agreement.

8.3 Exit interviews are conducted by HR on the employee's last day. Participation is voluntary but encouraged to help CloudFlow improve.

8.4 Non-compete agreements (where applicable and enforceable) are limited to 12 months and the specific business area the employee worked in.
