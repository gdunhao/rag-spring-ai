CloudFlow SaaS Platform — Customer FAQ
========================================

ACCOUNT & BILLING
-----------------

Q: How do I create a CloudFlow account?
A: Visit app.cloudflow.io/signup and enter your email, company name, and password. You'll receive a verification email within 5 minutes. Click the link to activate your account. Free trial requires no credit card.

Q: What pricing plans does CloudFlow offer?
A: CloudFlow offers three plans: Starter ($29/month for up to 5 users, 10GB storage), Professional ($79/month for up to 25 users, 100GB storage, priority support), and Enterprise (custom pricing for unlimited users, unlimited storage, dedicated account manager, SLA guarantees, and SSO).

Q: How do I upgrade my plan?
A: Go to Settings → Billing → Change Plan. Select your new plan and confirm. Upgrades take effect immediately, and you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle.

Q: Can I get a refund?
A: Yes, CloudFlow offers a 30-day money-back guarantee for all paid plans. Contact support@cloudflow.io within 30 days of your first payment. After 30 days, we offer prorated credits for annual plans cancelled mid-term.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Your account will remain active until the end of your current billing period. You can export all your data before cancellation via Settings → Data Export.

FEATURES & USAGE
----------------

Q: What file formats does CloudFlow support?
A: CloudFlow supports PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, CSV, JSON, XML, PNG, JPG, SVG, and MP4. Maximum file size is 500MB for Starter, 2GB for Professional, and 10GB for Enterprise plans.

Q: Does CloudFlow support team collaboration?
A: Yes! All plans include real-time collaboration features: shared workspaces, commenting, @mentions, task assignments, and activity feeds. Professional and Enterprise plans add video conferencing and screen sharing.

Q: Can I integrate CloudFlow with other tools?
A: CloudFlow integrates with 200+ tools including Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, Jira, GitHub, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier, and more. API access is available on Professional and Enterprise plans.

Q: Is there a mobile app?
A: Yes, CloudFlow has native iOS and Android apps available on the App Store and Google Play. The mobile apps support offline mode, push notifications, and document scanning.

Q: How does the AI assistant feature work?
A: CloudFlow's AI assistant (available on Professional and Enterprise plans) can summarize documents, generate reports, answer questions about your workspace data, and automate repetitive tasks. It uses your workspace context to provide relevant suggestions.

SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
---------------------

Q: Is my data encrypted?
A: Yes, CloudFlow uses AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. Enterprise plans offer customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK) and hardware security module (HSM) support.

Q: Is CloudFlow SOC 2 compliant?
A: Yes, CloudFlow is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and HIPAA ready (Enterprise plan with BAA). We undergo annual third-party security audits and publish transparency reports.

Q: Does CloudFlow support Single Sign-On (SSO)?
A: SSO via SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect is available on Enterprise plans. We support identity providers including Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, and OneLogin.

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: CloudFlow operates data centers in US-East (Virginia), EU-West (Ireland), and APAC (Singapore). Enterprise customers can choose their data residency region. All data centers are SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified.

TECHNICAL SUPPORT
-----------------

Q: How do I contact support?
A: Starter: Email support (support@cloudflow.io, 48-hour response time). Professional: Email + live chat (4-hour response time). Enterprise: Email + live chat + phone + dedicated account manager (1-hour response time for critical issues).

Q: Does CloudFlow have a status page?
A: Yes, visit status.cloudflow.io for real-time system status, incident reports, and scheduled maintenance notifications. You can subscribe to email or SMS alerts.

Q: Is there a knowledge base or documentation?
A: Yes, docs.cloudflow.io contains comprehensive guides, tutorials, API documentation, and video walkthroughs. We also have a community forum at community.cloudflow.io with over 10,000 active members.

Q: What is your uptime SLA?
A: Starter: 99.5% uptime. Professional: 99.9% uptime. Enterprise: 99.99% uptime with financial SLA credits for any downtime exceeding the guaranteed threshold.
